Strategy rules

Range = high/low of the 10 days before today, no wider than 4 × ATR20, with average volume below the 20 days before it. LONG when today closes above the range high in an uptrend (close > SMA50, SMA50 rising) on volume ≥ 1.5 × the range average; SHORT is the mirror. Enter at the next day's open. Stop = range midpoint, target = range high + range height (short: low − height), otherwise exit at the close of day 20. One position at a time per instrument. P&L per 1 futures lot (NIFTY 65, SENSEX 20) after 0.05 %/side costs. No orders are placed.
